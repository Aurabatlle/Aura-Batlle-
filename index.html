ABOUT US

Welcome to Aura Battle

Aura Battle is an Android platform focused on Free Fire MAX community tournaments. It helps players explore different match formats, understand participation rules and find tournament-related information in one place.

Players can explore solo, duo and squad formats. Tournament schedules, entry requirements, prizes and availability may vary, so always check the current details shown inside the app before joining a match.

Why Choose Aura Battle?

  • Multiple match formats
  • Support contact options
  • Tournament information
  • Published participation rules
  • Solo, duo and squad formats
  • Tournament updates
  • Game-specific rules
  • Community competition

TOURNAMENT RULES

Before joining

  • Read the complete rules and eligibility requirements for each tournament.
  • Check the match date, start time, team format and entry details in the app.
  • Use the correct in-game name and account details when registering.

During a match

  • Do not use cheats, hacks, scripts or unauthorized third-party tools.
  • Do not team up in modes where teaming is prohibited.
  • Follow the room instructions and decisions communicated by the tournament organiser.

Results and support

  • Follow the result-submission and verification process stated for the event.
  • Contact Aura Battle support if you have a question or need to report an issue.
  • The specific rules displayed for an individual tournament take precedence over this general summary.
